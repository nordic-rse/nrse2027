## Do you write code for research? {#about}

**Come and meet others who do too.**

Whether you work on analysis scripts, research tools, data pipelines, or software supporting a laboratory, this is a place to find peers across disciplines.

Research software engineering brings software skills into research. You don’t need to call yourself a research software engineer to attend the conference.

- Researchers and doctoral students who write code
- Developers building and maintaining research tools
- Data specialists and research-computing colleagues
- Laboratory staff working with software
- Established RSEs and people discovering the community

First-time participants and presenters are welcome. Come as an attendee or share a story from your work.

## Practical ideas. New connections. {#experience}

We are planning broad coverage through short contributions, with time to talk and learn from one another.

### Share what you know

Tools, scripts, teams, and lessons from making software work for research.

### Find your peers

Meet people across research disciplines who face similar challenges.

### Start a conversation

Bring a question, a work in progress, or an idea for collaboration.
